About Me

I'm an M.Sc. student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Dhaka and a Lecturer in the Department of CSE at Daffodil International University, where I supervise student research and teach courses spanning algorithms, compiler design, and database systems. I completed my B.Sc. in CSE at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET) in 2024.

My research centers on making vision-language models more trustworthy: detecting and reducing hallucination, studying moral sycophancy under social pressure, and building forensic tools for digital media forgery detection. I'm currently applying to PhD programs to continue this work at a larger scale.
